Competence centre registration – join the shared tools

One form, about five minutes. Tell us which tools your centre wants to work with and who validates your region's data. Nothing about your region is published before your centre has validated it.

What you can join
  • Value Chain Radar – a country layer for your region under the shared taxonomy, validated and co-branded by your centre.
  • IP Catalogue – IP and results of your centre and the pilot lines you work with, searchable by taxonomy code.
  • Cross-centre referrals – one shared case record, so a referral counts for both centres (home centre reports, host records).
  • Pillar I Radar (alpha), Silicon Atlas scenarios and taxonomy & radar training for your team.
